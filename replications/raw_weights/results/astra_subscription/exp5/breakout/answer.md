First-layer units encode candidate landing positions, motion direction, paddle-bounce direction, and predicted row-3 brick collisions. Second-layer units select the appropriate forecast and steer toward it. The paddle settles early; on the final descending step, it randomizes between safe catches to diversify subsequent trajectories.
